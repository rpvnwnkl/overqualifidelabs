About

Original company introduction

Started as a side project in the winter of 2009, OVERQUALIFIDE Labs soon became the project. OVERQUALIFIDE is an idea of a company. An idea that balances form and content in an effort to create high-quality functional products. With a little bit of research, experience, conversation, and thorough reflection, the Lab attempts to produce items that reflect the company's core values of quality, honesty, and integrity. Real American values; values that this country was founded on.
