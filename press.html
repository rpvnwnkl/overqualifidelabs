OVERQUALIFIDE Labs presents the OQ No-Name Not Real Cool Skateboard Deck Series

June 16, 2010 · FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

BOSTON - OVERQUALIFIDE Labs is producing, in conjunction with a premier skateboard manufacturer, a limited number of handcrafted skateboard decks.

Each deck will feature an authentic OVERQUALIFIDE design as well as a one of a kind serialized brass tag and a certificate of authenticity. This certificate of authenticity will guarantee the holder that their skateboard deck is uniquely handcrafted by OVERQUALIFIDE Labs’ lead skateboard artisan and adheres to OVERQUALIFIDE Labs’ meticulous production standards.

Every new OVERQUALIFIDE skateboard deck is also covered by an original owner Lifetime Guarantee against breakage. Log onto OVERQUALIFIDE.com to learn more details about their Lifetime Guarantee.

Proceeds from each OVERQUALIFIDE skateboard deck will be donated to local and national charities that foster the arts and education.

The OVERQUALIFIDE decks will be available for immediate purchase directly through OVERQUALIFIDE.com as well as fine retailers throughout the country later this fall.

About OVERQUALIFIDE Labs

OVERQUALIFIDE Labs, LLC. is an artistic research center located in Boston, Massachusetts. The Lab focuses on creating high quality art objects for the tastefully educated consumer.

The Lab was formed in January of 2010 by the designer Matthew Mills, formerly with the General Electric Company.
